Interview Process

Everything that happens before your first question

Seven steps stand between you and the interview. Here's exactly what each one involves so there are no surprises on the day.

1

Sign In to Your Account

Log in with the email and password you registered with. If you don't have an account yet, visit letshyre.com to create a candidate profile. Your account holds your interview slot, profile photo, and results.

2

Start from Your Dashboard

After signing in you'll land on your personal dashboard showing your profile and available interview slots. Click Take Interview to begin — the app immediately runs a background security check before anything else opens.

3

Clear Your Environment

The security scanner checks for applications that could compromise exam integrity — web browsers, screen recorders, AI assistants, and video-call tools. Any flagged app must be closed before you can proceed. The check re-runs until your environment is clean.

Tip: close all other apps before clicking Take Interview
4

Grant Device Permissions

You'll be prompted to allow access to your microphone, camera, and screen. All three are required for live proctoring. Grant each permission when your operating system asks — the interview cannot proceed without them.

5

Verify Your Identity

Read a short statement aloud for voice registration, then allow the camera to take a live photo. Your live image is compared against the profile photo you uploaded when creating your account. If the match fails, you'll be prompted to retake the photo in better lighting.

Tip: face the camera directly in a well-lit room
6

Choose Your Interview Role

Select the position you're interviewing for. You can proceed with the role assigned during onboarding, or choose a different slot if multiple are available to you. The question set is tailored to the role you select here.

7

Begin Your Interview

You're all set. The interview window opens in full-screen with screen recording active. Answer each question within the allocated time — you can navigate between questions before the session timer runs out. Stay in the window at all times; navigating away counts as a violation.

Ready to get started?

Sign in to your account and click Take Interview when you're prepared.
