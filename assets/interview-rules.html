Before you begin

This interview is proctored through your camera and screen. Here is what the proctoring looks for.

These count as a strike

  • Leaving the interview window, switching tabs or exiting fullscreen
  • Someone else in view, or nobody in view
  • A phone or another prohibited device in view
  • Apps, screen sharing or extra displays the desktop app blocks

These only give you a warning

  • Your face briefly leaving the frame
  • Poor lighting or an unclear view of your face
  • Your camera pausing for a moment

The limits

    Your interview timer starts as soon as the interview opens.
