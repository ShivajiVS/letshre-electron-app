Security Checks

We check that nothing else on this computer can see, share or help with your interview. Close anything we find, then continue.

These checks make sure only this app can see or share your screen during the interview.

  • External Displays Only one screen can be in use.
  • Meeting Apps Video call apps can share your screen or audio.
  • Screen Recording & Sharing Recorders and streaming apps can capture the interview.
  • Remote Access & Casting These let another device see or control this one.
  • Web Browsers The interview runs inside this app, so browsers need to be closed.
  • AI Assistant Apps These apps can suggest answers during an interview.
  • Deep System Scan Looks for hidden overlays and automation tools the other checks can miss.
